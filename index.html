About

A vocalist focused on emotion, phrasing, and memorable delivery.

Ralskies is a singer whose style sits between theatrical performance and modern online music culture. The strongest material is music that benefits from expressive vocals, melodic shape, and a sense of feeling in the performance instead of something flat or generic.

Best Fit

The best collaborations come from a clear sonic direction, a defined vocal role, and a real reason the voice fits the song. If you reach out, include the genre, timeline, budget, and what kind of vocal part you actually need.

Open To

What to send if you want the best chance of a yes.

Good Pitch

Clear style, real timeline, and a defined vocal role.

Strong inquiries usually include a demo, references, deadline, budget, and a direct explanation of what kind of performance the song needs.

Less Useful

Vague asks with no direction, references, or scope.

If the pitch is just “want to collab?” with no concept, no material, and no clear ask, it is much harder to review or prioritize.
